# GeezerFit Privacy Policy

**Developer:** sofToolsmith Studio
**Contact:** dave@softoolsmith.com
**Effective date:** November 13, 2026

GeezerFit is a workout checklist app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **the app does not collect, sell, or share your personal information.**

## What stays on your device

Your workout history, the weights you enter, your unit preference (lbs/feet or kg/meters), and any in-progress workout are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We (the developer) never receive this data, and the app has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

## Network use

The app downloads workout plan files (small text files) from a public GitHub repository (`raw.githubusercontent.com/davecove/GeezerFit`) when it starts or when you tap "Check for new plans". Nothing about you or your workouts is sent in these requests. As with any web request, GitHub may see your device's IP address and request details under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We do not receive or control that information.

## Backups

If Android's device backup is turned on, your phone may include the app's data (workout history and settings) in your Google account backup, so it can be restored on a new device. That backup is handled by Google and governed by your Google account settings and Google's privacy policy. It is not accessible to us.

## Exporting your data

The Settings screen lets you export your history as a CSV or JSON file. The file is created on your device and shared only through the Android share sheet to the app you choose. We do not receive it.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** to download workout plans.
- **Vibrate:** to buzz when a rest timer ends.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

## Children

GeezerFit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Not medical advice

Workout plans and coaching notes in the app are general fitness information, not medical advice. Check with a healthcare professional before starting an exercise program.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your device. You can also clear it in Android Settings, under Apps, GeezerFit, Storage, Clear data. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete. If you used Android backup, you can manage or delete that backup in your Google account.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
